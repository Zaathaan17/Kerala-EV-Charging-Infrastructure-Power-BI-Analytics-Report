# ⚡ Kerala EV Charging Infrastructure Analytics

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Kerala's EV charging ecosystem across all 14 districts, uncover infrastructure distribution and utilization patterns, and provide insights into charging demand, network growth, and potential expansion areas.

## 🔍 Key Insights & Analysis

* **District Infrastructure:** Ernakulam has the highest number of charging stations with **236 stations**, followed by Thiruvananthapuram and Thrissur.

* **Charging Capacity:** The network contains **7,219 charging points**, with DC Fast CCS2 representing the largest share of charging capacity.

* **Network Utilization:** Kerala's overall estimated charging network utilization stands at **64.17%**.

* **Charging Demand:** The dashboard records approximately **48,948 estimated daily charging sessions** across the network.

* **Energy Consumption:** Estimated daily energy consumption reaches approximately **2.14 million kWh** across the analyzed charging infrastructure.

* **District Utilization:** District-level utilization varies across Kerala, allowing high- and relatively low-utilization areas to be compared.

* **Charger Technology:** The dashboard compares AC 7.4kW, AC Type 2, DC Fast CCS2, DC Fast CHAdeMO, and DC Ultra Fast CCS2 charging technologies.

* **Area-wise Utilization:** Charging utilization is compared across **Rural, Semi-Urban, Suburban, and Urban** locations.

* **Network Growth:** Charging-point growth is analyzed according to the station opening year to understand the development of Kerala's charging infrastructure.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. State Overview
Provides a high-level view of Kerala's EV charging ecosystem, including:

* Total stations
* Charging points
* Daily energy consumption
* Daily charging sessions
* Average utilization
* Station status
* Site-type distribution
* District-wise station distribution

### 2. District Analysis
Provides a district-level comparison of:

* Charging stations
* Charging ports
* Daily charging sessions
* Average utilization
* Daily charging energy

### 3. Charging Network
Analyzes the structure and technology of Kerala's charging network through:

* AC vs DC charging points
* Charger types
* Connector types
* Charger technology mix
* Maximum charging power
* Charging network growth by year

### 4. Utilization & Demand
Focuses on charging demand and infrastructure utilization:

* Average daily sessions per station
* Average daily energy consumption
* Overall network utilization
* High-demand charging stations
* Area-wise utilization
* Utilization trend by opening year

### 5. Strategic Insights
Combines major infrastructure and utilization indicators to provide a comparative district-level view of:

* Charging infrastructure
* Charging capacity
* Daily demand
* Utilization
* Energy consumption

The dashboard uses DAX measures to dynamically calculate important KPIs such as:

* Total Charging Stations
* Total Charging Points
* Total Daily Sessions
* Total Daily Energy
* Average Network Utilization
* Average Power
* Average Daily Sessions per Station
* District-level performance metrics

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform EV charging infrastructure data into an interactive analytical dashboard that provides a clear understanding of:

* Where charging infrastructure is concentrated
* How charging infrastructure is being utilized
* Which charger technologies are being deployed
* How charging demand varies across districts
* How the charging network has developed over time
* Where infrastructure gaps and expansion opportunities may exist
